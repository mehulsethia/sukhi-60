## 9. FAQ draft

> **Note:** FAQ wording below is proposed supporting content for the landing page, not a claim of capabilities that have already shipped. Remove or update any item that does not match the release.

**Q: What is Sukhi?**  
A: Sukhi is a personal wellbeing companion that helps you build simple daily routines, check in on your progress and find a way forward when you miss a day.

**Q: How do I update Sukhi?**  
A: You can tap Done, type a short update or use your voice. Optional photo sharing may also be available.

**Q: What happens if I miss a day?**  
A: Sukhi keeps a record of your check-ins and can suggest a gentler way to restart. One missed day never erases your progress.

**Q: Can family members see my activity?**  
A: Family sharing is optional. You decide what information is shared, subject to the privacy controls available in your version of Sukhi.

**Q: Is Sukhi a substitute for my doctor?**  
A: No. Sukhi supports everyday wellbeing routines and does not provide medical diagnosis or replace professional advice.

**Typeface:** Inter for English; Noto Sans Devanagari for Hindi/Marathi.  
**Minimum body text:** Prefer 16–18 px.  
**Interaction targets:** At least 44–48 px.  
**Contrast:** Test against WCAG AA, including teal/white button pairings.  
**Illustration:** One restrained sunrise/pathway hero drawing; avoid decorative graphics on each card.  
**Mobile:** Read hero before image, stack sections, never make the headline illegibly small.
